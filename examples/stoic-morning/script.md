# Before the day starts

<!-- Format: vertical motivation short. Original text in a Stoic spirit; no quotes from real people. -->

<!-- Each paragraph is one scene. The `image:` line under it describes the picture for that
     scene; it is never narrated. Delete a line to let the narration be the prompt instead. -->

Today you will meet people who are rude, impatient and ungrateful.
<!-- image: a crowded city street of blurred rushing people, one calm marble statue standing still in the middle -->

That is not a surprise. It is the weather.
<!-- image: dark storm clouds gathering over ancient stone columns, dramatic side light -->

You do not control the weather. You control whether you bring a coat.
<!-- image: a marble statue of a robed philosopher calmly holding a cloak in falling rain -->

So decide now, before the day decides for you.
<!-- image: a marble statue's hand closing into a firm fist, first light of dawn across the stone -->

Do the work in front of you. Say less than you want to.
<!-- image: a stonemason's hands carving a block of marble with hammer and chisel, dust in a beam of light -->

And tonight, ask one question: was I useful?
<!-- image: a marble statue of a seated thinker beside a small oil lamp at night, long shadows -->

That is the whole practice. Begin.
<!-- image: sunrise breaking over a mountain ridge behind the silhouette of a classical statue -->
