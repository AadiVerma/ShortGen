# The Lighthouse Keeper

<!-- A sample script. Headings and comments are not narrated.
     Each paragraph becomes at least one scene. -->

Mira had kept the lighthouse for eleven winters. Every evening she climbed
the ninety steps and lit the great lamp.

One night a storm rolled in faster than any she had seen. The wind tore at
the shutters, and the lamp went dark.

Far out on the black water, a small fishing boat was losing its way. Its
captain searched the horizon and saw nothing at all.

Mira did not wait for the storm to pass. She carried a single lantern up
the stairs, held it against the glass, and stood there until her arms shook.

Just before dawn, the boat slid safely into the harbor. Its captain never
learned how small the light had been.

Mira relit the great lamp, and went down to make tea.
