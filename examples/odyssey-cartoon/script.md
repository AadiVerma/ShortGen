# The Odyssey in one minute

<!-- Format: cartoon myth explainer. One paragraph = one scene = one picture. -->

<!-- Each paragraph is one scene. The `image:` line under it describes the picture for that
     scene; it is never narrated. Delete a line to let the narration be the prompt instead. -->

Meet Odysseus, king of a small island called Ithaca.
<!-- image: a bearded Greek warrior in a bronze helmet with a red crest and a red cape, standing proudly on a small green island with one white house, calm blue sea, golden morning sky -->

He won a ten-year war with one clever trick: a giant wooden horse.
<!-- image: a giant wooden horse on wheels outside the stone walls of a burning ancient city at dusk, tiny soldiers watching -->

Winning was the easy part. Getting home took ten more years.
<!-- image: a small Greek sailing ship with a striped sail alone on a wide sea at sunset, a far-off island on the horizon -->

First, a one-eyed giant trapped his crew in a cave and started eating them.
<!-- image: a huge one-eyed giant in a dark cave holding up a tiny frightened sailor, other small sailors cowering near the cave mouth -->

Odysseus blinded the giant and escaped by clinging under its sheep.
<!-- image: a bearded Greek warrior in a bronze helmet with a red crest and a red cape clinging underneath a large fluffy sheep walking out of a cave, a giant hand patting the sheep's back, green hillside -->

Then came the Sirens, whose song pulled sailors onto the rocks.
<!-- image: three winged women singing on a jagged rock in a purple twilight sea, musical notes drifting toward a small ship, wrecks on the rocks -->

He plugged his crew's ears with wax and had himself tied to the mast.
<!-- image: a bearded Greek warrior in a bronze helmet with a red crest and a red cape tied with ropes to the mast of a ship, straining forward, two calm sailors rowing beside him -->

Storms, monsters and an angry sea god took every ship and every man he had.
<!-- image: a furious bearded sea god with a trident rising from dark stormy waves, lightning, a shattered ship, one small man on a raft -->

Twenty years after he left, he washed ashore at home, disguised as a beggar.
<!-- image: an old hooded beggar in a ragged brown cloak leaning on a staff, walking up a beach toward a white palace with columns, warm light -->

His wife Penelope had waited the whole time, fending off a hall full of suitors.
<!-- image: a calm queen in a purple dress weaving at a tall loom in a palace hall, a rowdy crowd of men feasting behind her -->

One impossible shot with his old bow proved who he was. The king was home.
<!-- image: a bearded king with a gold crown drawing a great bow in a palace hall, an arrow flying through a row of axe heads, golden light -->
