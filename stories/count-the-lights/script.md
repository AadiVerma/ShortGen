# Do not count the lights

<!-- Format: original horror micro-story, told in under forty seconds.
     Fiction, written for this project. The numbers are never shown in the pictures on purpose: the image model
     cannot count, and the viewer's imagination does it better. -->

My grandmother had one rule about the lake house.
<!-- image: a small old wooden house on the shore of a dark still lake at dusk, one window lit -->

When the fog comes in, close the curtains. And do not count the lights on the water.
<!-- image: thick white fog rolling across a black lake toward the shore, bare trees -->
<!-- camera: pan_left -->

Last night, the fog came in.
<!-- image: a window at night with heavy curtains half open, pale fog pressing against the glass -->

I counted. Seven lanterns, drifting toward the shore.
<!-- image: a line of small orange lantern lights floating on black water in fog, seen from far away -->
<!-- camera: zoom_in -->

This morning there were wet footprints on the porch. Seven pairs.
<!-- image: wet bare footprints on the grey boards of an old wooden porch, leading to a closed door, cold morning light -->

Tonight, the fog is back.
<!-- image: the dark lake house swallowed by fog at night, only the faint outline of its roof visible -->

I can see the lanterns again.
<!-- image: orange lantern lights on black water in fog, much closer now, their reflections long on the water -->
<!-- camera: zoom_in -->

There are eight.
<!-- image: a single orange lantern glowing right outside a fogged window at night, very close -->
<!-- camera: zoom_in -->
