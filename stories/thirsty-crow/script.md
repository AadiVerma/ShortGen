# The thirsty crow

<!-- Format: a traditional fable for young children (about 3 to 7), retold in new words.
     The story is Aesop's "The Crow and the Pitcher", which is ancient and in the public
     domain, and is one of the most retold children's stories on YouTube. The telling,
     the wording and the pictures here are this project's own: no existing video's
     script, characters, songs or look are copied.

     It uses what the most-watched children's videos have in common: a problem in the
     first second, sounds to copy, words repeated in threes, a question with a moment
     to think before the answer, and a short lesson.

     The crow's look is set once, under `characters:` in config.yaml, and is added to
     every scene whose narration says "crow". -->

Uh oh! This little crow is so thirsty. The sun is hot, hot, hot, and there is no water.
<!-- image: close-up of the little crow looking at the viewer, tired, a big bright sun, dry yellow field -->
<!-- camera: zoom_in -->

Look! A pot! The crow hops up and peeks inside. There is water! But it is way down at the bottom.
<!-- image: the little crow perched on the rim of a tall brown clay pot, peeking down into it, sunny field -->

The crow stretches her beak. Too short! She pushes the pot. Too heavy! What would you do?
<!-- image: the little crow leaning deep into a tall clay pot, only its tail feathers sticking out, sunny field -->

Then the crow sees some little pebbles. And she gets an idea!
<!-- image: the little crow looking down at small round grey pebbles on the ground, bright sparkles above its head -->
<!-- camera: zoom_in -->

The crow drops one pebble in. Plop! Then another. Plop! Then another. Plop! Plop! Plop!
<!-- image: the little crow holding a round pebble in its beak above a tall clay pot, a splash of water drops -->

Look! The water climbs up, up, up, all the way to the top.
<!-- image: a tall brown clay pot filled to the brim with sparkling blue water, round pebbles around it, sunny field -->
<!-- camera: pan_up -->

The crow drinks, and drinks. Ahh! Clever little crow!
<!-- image: the happy little crow drinking from a clay pot full of sparkling water, bright sunny day -->

When something is hard, do not give up. Think, and try, little by little.
<!-- image: a tiny round fluffy black baby crow chick with huge shiny eyes flying happily, sunny sky with fluffy white clouds -->
<!-- camera: zoom_out -->

What would you put in the pot? Tell a grown-up!
<!-- image: close-up of a tiny round fluffy black baby crow chick with huge shiny eyes looking at the viewer, sunny field -->
