# Golden repair

<!-- Format: short reflection. Kintsugi ("golden joinery") is the Japanese practice of
     mending broken pottery with lacquer dusted with powdered gold. -->

In Japan, when a bowl breaks, it is not always thrown away.
<!-- image: a broken ceramic tea bowl in several pieces on a dark wooden table, soft window light -->

The cracks are filled with lacquer, and dusted with gold.
<!-- image: a fine brush tracing a thin line of gold along a crack in a pale ceramic bowl, extreme close-up -->
<!-- camera: pan_right -->

It is called kintsugi. Golden joinery.
<!-- image: a pale ceramic bowl mended with bright veins of gold, sitting alone on a dark wooden table -->

The repair is not hidden. It becomes the most beautiful part.
<!-- image: extreme close-up of a glowing golden seam running across pale glazed ceramic -->
<!-- camera: zoom_in -->

You have cracks too. Losses. Mistakes. Bad years.
<!-- image: a dark ceramic statue of a human head and shoulders seen from behind, fine cracks across its surface -->

You do not have to hide them.
<!-- image: the same dark ceramic figure seen from behind, its cracks now filled with bright glowing gold -->

Fill them with gold.
<!-- image: a ceramic bowl with golden seams holding steaming tea in warm morning sunlight -->
<!-- camera: zoom_out -->
