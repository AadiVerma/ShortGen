# The town that has been on fire since 1962

<!-- Format: strange true history. Facts from History.com's account of Centralia,

There is a town in Pennsylvania that has been on fire since 1962.
<!-- image: an empty small town street at dusk with thin white smoke rising from the ground, bare trees -->

It is thought to have started at the town dump, where burning rubbish lit the coal underneath.
<!-- image: a glowing red seam of burning coal deep underground, dark layers of rock above it, a few small houses on the surface -->
<!-- camera: pan_down -->

That coal has been burning beneath the streets ever since.
<!-- image: tunnels of glowing orange embers running under a quiet town, seen in cross-section -->

Smoke rose through cracks in the road. The ground grew warm enough to melt the snow.
<!-- image: a long crack splitting an empty asphalt road, smoke pouring out of it, snow melted in a strip beside it -->
<!-- camera: zoom_in -->

The government decided it was cheaper to move the people than to put the fire out.
<!-- image: a row of empty wooden houses with boarded windows on a foggy street, an old moving truck driving away -->

Almost everyone left. A handful refused.
<!-- image: one small house with a single lit window standing alone among empty overgrown lots at night -->

Even its zip code was taken away.
<!-- image: an old rusted mailbox leaning in tall grass beside an empty overgrown road -->

And the fire could keep burning for another hundred years.
